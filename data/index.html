Tonmick ordinary data

Tonmick ordinary data for The Claim Ceiling

All records are fictional. These files are selected, verified projections of Tonmick World ordinary records, not a newly generated sample. They support the cases named below. See DICTIONARY.csv and PROVENANCE.json. Use student_id for individual-record joins; row inventories differ by purpose.

File Rows and use
ordinary/baseline-items.csv 1,219 baseline respondents; raw stress/wellbeing items. S5 requires all ten items and reverses item four on each scale.
ordinary/baseline-offer.csv The same respondents, with college and randomized offer; offer is assignment, not attendance.
ordinary/observed-registry.csv 1,600 original invitees under the actual policy mixture, including 11 unknown outcomes. Use withdrawal_label with outcome_known, not a legacy zero.
ordinary/month3-stress.csv Month-three response and raw stress items for baseline respondents. Missing response/items remain missing. The retest has its own eligibility and scoring rule.
ordinary/visits-exposure.csv Archived visits, recorded enrollment months and named covariates for baseline respondents. Recording and postassignment exposure limit interpretation.
ordinary/month3-screen.csv All invitees retained before the exit_month > 3 landmark. Intention is a binary month-three response; blank means no available screen.
ordinary/synthesis-colleges.csv Sixteen anonymized college summaries. GPA known counts differ from withdrawal-known counts. Grade sums are integer hundredths; sums of squares use those same integer units squared.

The registry endpoint is withdrawal before competing transfer by month twelve. Consent withdrawal leaves the label unknown. The month-three screen uses only records still observed and event-free at that scheduled landmark. The college synthesis is an analogue within one study, not sixteen independent replicated studies.

The private World registry also contains simulator truth and author-only extensions. They are absent from this public selection. The public checks use these data and declared working-model inputs; inherited fits and upstream forecast evidence have separately stated scopes.
