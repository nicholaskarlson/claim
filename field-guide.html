Prompts for the eight kinds of claim

Claim kind First entries on the card
Descriptive Eligible units, scoring or pairing rule, numerator and denominator, unknown records
Population Named frame, entry and response mechanism, support, observation assumptions
Causal Policies, outcome horizon, population, assignment or adjustment, interference and follow-up
Model-based Parameter and scale, mean and variance, dependence, reference law or prior
Construct Intended interpretation, scoring, reliability, structure, comparability and validity evidence
Change Occasions, paired group, selection, attrition, scale meaning and comparison
Predictive Target, time of prediction, frozen rule, comparator, scoring and evaluation history
Cumulative Eligible settings or studies, estimand, dependence, heterogeneity and prediction assumptions

When a method is unfamiliar, add target, link, reduced example, independent check, failure test and rewritten sentence. The learning cases demonstrate that route. The coverage register specifies what the inspected editions teach.

Keep the units in the sentence

A percentage point is a difference between percentages; a rate ratio is a ratio under a defined rate model. A completion range varies unknown records; a confidence interval comes from a procedure under its assumptions. A posterior probability conditions on a model and prior; BF01 compares evidence under specified H0 and H1.

Save the sentence with the evidence version that supported it. State one next check that could change its interpretation.
