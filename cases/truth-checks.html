What the closing truth audit can check

Sentence Completed check Remaining match
S1 Assigned no-offer counts and known-label rule The correctness of recorded labels is an assumption
S2 Original invitation-roster completion bounds Wider enrollment or system frame not admitted
S3 Conditional finite design analysis Matching potential-outcome benchmark not admitted
S4 Archived visit models and named uncertainty Matching generator contrast not admitted; a p value is not a generator parameter
S5 Observed score calculation and prior one-state recovery check Joint stress–wellbeing generating benchmark not admitted
S6 Frozen evaluation on later simulated outcomes Further system performance and intervention benefit require their own evidence

The closing addendum uses ordinary records and previously opened evidence. It opens no new hidden record and makes no claim to have measured the general success rate of a reporting rule. A future benchmark must match the target, eligible units, population, policy, scale and occasion, with exposure and comparison rules documented.
