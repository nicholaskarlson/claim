Book 9 Unit 4’s standardized working estimate for withdrawal by month twelve before transfer was −2.41 percentage points (offer minus no offer). The 95% model interval was −6.21 to +1.39, and the matching 90% interval was −5.60 to +0.78. The two-sided difference test gave p = 0.214. The 90% interval crosses the lower teaching margin of −2 points, so the equivalence test gives p = 0.584. The model uses known labels and a fixed standardization roster.
