Follow the six steps

Name the target, locate its limiting link, reduce to an inspectable example, calculate independently, test a failure, and write the bounded claim. These teaching cases have known prior exposure and no new held-out evaluation.

Month-three intention screen

Among 995 eligible Tonmick entrants with a month-three intention response and a known outcome, the hit rate was 65.35% and the false-alarm rate 10.18%. There were 1,485 eligible entrants and 1,002 with a screen, including 7 unknown outcomes. The four known-label cells are TP 66, FP 91, FN 35 and TN 803. Screen coverage was 67.47%. Positive predictive value was 42.04%.

The uncorrected probit difference is 1.666 and criterion 0.438 under the equal-variance normal representation. One binary operating point cannot test that latent distribution. Completing the unknown labels gives hit-rate bounds 61.68% to 65.69% and positive-predictive-value bounds 41.77% to 42.41%. These are missing-label bounds conditional on known labels, not confidence limits or a policy benefit.

Normal working comparison

Under the fixed normal working likelihood and a zero-centered normal prior with SD three percentage points, the posterior probability within H1 of a reduction beyond two points is 42.7%. BF01 is 1.068 for the point null against that proper two-sided alternative. Posterior probabilities condition on H1. The likelihood is the fixed-SE normal approximation for Chapter 9’s Book 9 adjusted and standardized contrast in percentage points.
