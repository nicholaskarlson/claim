The 28 topics proposed in the commissioning outline, reconciled against inspected Book 9, Book 10 and current Book 11 sources. Statuses describe this reader’s documented route. Books 12–15 and the Chapter 14 cases remain prospective.

Read the status with the route

Taught. A worked case with declared data, computation checks, problems and full solutions is available in an inspected source.

Named. Defined here or in an inspected source, with a question and a route for further work.

Boundary. Outside the worked scope documented for this reader, with the additional analysis or evidence specified.

Planned. A future case or volume is proposed and still awaits its completed checks.

R01. MCMC and Bayesian structural or synthesis models

Status. Boundary.

Scope. Posterior sampling needs convergence and Monte Carlo error checks beyond the deterministic examples inspected here.

Audit question. Which posterior quantity is estimated, and how large is its simulation error?

Further route. Stan posterior-analysis diagnostics; reduce the model to an analytic or grid example.

Evidence. Commissioned Book 11 outline, Chapter 13 register. Book 9 opening, worked scope. Stan Reference Manual, Posterior Analysis.

R02. Tree ensembles, boosting and neural networks

Status. Boundary.

Scope. The current forecast example uses a frozen enrollment model. Training and tuning another learner need a separate contract.

Audit question. Were preprocessing and tuning confined to development, and how was the frozen forecast scored?

Further route. Chapter 8 scoring and Chapter 10 provenance; specialist training and validation.

Evidence. Commissioned Book 11 outline, Chapter 13 register. Book 9 opening, worked scope. Breiman (2001), Random Forests. Book 11 Chapter 10, verification.

R04. WLSMV and limited-information ordinal estimation

Status. Named.

Further route. Book 10 Unit 6 and Flora and Curran (2004).

Evidence. Commissioned Book 11 outline, Chapter 13 register. Book 10 Unit 6, ordinal estimators. Flora & Curran (2004), alternative estimators for ordinal CFA.

R27. Signal detection theory

Status. Taught.

Scope. Chapter 14 works the admitted month-three intention screen, its eligible frame, rates, missing-label bounds and a conditional probit representation, with an invented boundary example and three calculation routes. It does not teach the full range of SDT models or validate a latent distribution.

Audit question. What do the hit and false-alarm rates and latent assumptions allow the screen to claim?

Further route. Chapter 14 Case A, Book 10 Unit 10 for the observed screen, and Stanislaw and Todorov (1999) for further SDT methods.

Evidence. Commissioned Book 11 outline, Chapter 13 register. Book 11 Chapter 14, scoped learning cases.

R28. Bayes factors

Status. Taught.

Scope. Chapter 14 works a point-null versus proper normal-prior comparison using the declared Gaussian likelihood approximation from Chapter 9, three prior scales, conditional posterior probabilities and a verified toy example. This is a scoped normal case, not every Bayes factor or a finite design effect.

Audit question. Which two predictive models, including their priors, are being compared?

Further route. Chapter 14 Case B; the Bayesian volume for modeling and decision foundations; Kass and Raftery (1995) for model evidence and sensitivity.

Evidence. Commissioned Book 11 outline, Chapter 13 register. Book 11 Chapter 14, scoped learning cases.
