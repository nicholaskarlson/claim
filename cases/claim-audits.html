S1. Who was assigned no offer?

Field Completed audit
Original sentence and claim kind Description. “Fifteen percent of students without mentoring withdrew in their first year.”
Question and intended use How much withdrawal did the registry record in the arm assigned no offer?
Target, population, comparison and horizon The original no-offer arm, through month 12. The assigned-arm fraction and the known-label fraction answer separate questions.
Unit, observation and missingness rule One randomized baseline respondent. Retain all 610 assigned when reporting recorded events. The known-label denominator is 603.
Measurement or outcome definition Withdrawal before a competing transfer by month 12. Consent withdrawal can leave the outcome unknown.
Design and assignment or sampling rule College-blocked assignment defines the arm. Attendance can differ from assignment. This card describes one arm.
Estimation and precision Recorded events per assigned respondent 93/610 (15.2%). Among known labels 93/603 (15.4%). Binary completion gives 15.2% to 16.4% for the assigned-arm fraction.
Verification and source versions Chapter 2 checks respondent keys, assignment, registry labels and both denominators. Contract B11-S1-OBS-v1. Evidence and reproduction checks.
Transport, if the sentence travels Only the original assigned no-offer arm is described.
Limiting evidence The phrase “without mentoring” can imply attendance. Unknown labels leave the full-arm fraction partly unresolved.

S1 rewrite. Of the 610 baseline respondents assigned no mentoring offer, 93 had a recorded withdrawal before transfer by month 12 and 7 outcomes were unknown. Among the 603 with a known outcome, 93 withdrew.

Next study or check. Plan permitted registry follow-up through month 12 and document which outcomes still cannot be observed.

S2. Which students does the percentage represent?

Field Completed audit
Original sentence and claim kind Population description. “About fifteen percent of first-year students at these colleges withdraw.”
Question and intended use What can the registry say about withdrawal across the invitation roster?
Target, population, comparison and horizon The finite Tonmick invitation roster under the study’s actual mixture of policies. Each invitee counts once.
Unit, observation and missingness rule One invitee, including survey nonrespondents. The registry had 11 unknown labels.
Measurement or outcome definition Recorded withdrawal before transfer by month 12. Transfer first is a known competing outcome.
Design and assignment or sampling rule Use the original invitation list and its registry coverage. The colleges and invitees do not supply a probability sample of a broader system.
Estimation and precision Combine known events with binary limits on the unknown labels. The range reflects observation uncertainty, with known labels held fixed.
Verification and source versions Chapter 5 reconciles the registry with Book 8 and distinguishes this bound from its conditional event-time estimate. Contract B11-O5-FRAME-v1. Evidence and reproduction checks.
Transport, if the sentence travels All enrollees, non-invited students and later years require a matching frame and observation process.
Limiting evidence Invited students and all first-year enrollees are different populations.

S2 rewrite. In the 1,600-person Tonmick invitation roster, 257 withdrawals before transfer by month 12 were recorded and 11 outcomes were unknown. Holding the known labels fixed, the roster withdrawal fraction ranges from 16.06% to 16.75%.

Next study or check. Compare a defined all-enrollee roster with the invitation list and measure withdrawal coverage among those left out.

S3. What did the offer change?

Field Completed audit
Original sentence and claim kind Causal policy contrast. “Mentoring reduced withdrawal by about two percentage points.”
Question and intended use Would offering mentoring change withdrawal in the randomized baseline cohort?
Target, population, comparison and horizon Finite average effect of assignment to an offer versus no offer on month-12 withdrawal before transfer.
Unit, observation and missingness rule One randomized baseline respondent. All 1,219 remain in the target, including those with unknown labels.
Measurement or outcome definition A binary withdrawal outcome before transfer by month 12. The intervention is the offer.
Design and assignment or sampling rule Conditional on fixed offer counts, assignment is uniform within each college and independent across colleges. The effect also requires consistency and no interference.
Estimation and precision College-roster weighting with the realized assignment gives a completion range of −3.25 to −1.45 percentage points. The conservative design interval also allows assignment variation.
Verification and source versions Chapter 6 checks both completion extrema and the variance bound. Chapter 9 gives the separate Book 9 model comparison. Contract B11-D6-OFFER-v1. Evidence and reproduction checks.
Transport, if the sentence travels The finite cohort effect concerns the original study policies and colleges.
Limiting evidence The finite-effect interval leaves a reduction, zero and an increase compatible with the design calculation.

S3 rewrite. Under the stated assignment and outcome assumptions, the conservative 95% design interval for the effect of a mentoring offer on withdrawal before transfer by month 12 is −16.15 to +11.45 percentage points in the randomized cohort.

Next study or check. Plan a new blocked offer study with more colleges and more complete permitted outcome follow-up, sized for a predeclared worthwhile reduction.

S4. What did the service count capture?

Field Completed audit
Original sentence and claim kind Conditional model comparison. “Mentoring increased help-seeking by 13 percent (p < .001).”
Question and intended use How did the fitted rates of archived support visits compare at common recorded enrollment time and covariates?
Target, population, comparison and horizon Adjusted offer-to-no-offer ratio of fitted rates for archived visits while enrolled through month 12.
Unit, observation and missingness rule One randomized baseline respondent contributes the archived visit count and recorded enrollment months.
Measurement or outcome definition A visit in the student-support archive. Help sought elsewhere or unrecorded contact can escape the count.
Design and assignment or sampling rule Offer assignment was blocked by college. Enrollment time follows assignment. The offset conditions the comparison on exposure.
Estimation and precision The Poisson fit adjusts for prior grade, part-time status and first-generation status. The row-level and college-grouped covariance calculations use the same mean fit.
Verification and source versions Chapter 7 checks the Book 9 fits, the recorded exposure and the college-grouped covariance. Contract B11-M7-VISITS-v1. Evidence and reproduction checks.
Transport, if the sentence travels Other services and colleges can use different capture rules and have different enrollment patterns.
Limiting evidence The archive measures recorded use. Working intervals differ in whether they include a rate ratio of one.

S4 rewrite. For archived support visits, the adjusted Poisson offer rate ratio was 1.135 at common covariates and recorded enrollment time. The working 95% intervals were 0.990 to 1.300 with row-level robust uncertainty and 1.005 to 1.281 with the approximate college-grouped calculation.

Next study or check. Use a fixed follow-up window and identical recording across services in a study of the offer’s effect on cumulative use.

S5. Are those scores or underlying states?

Field Completed audit
Original sentence and claim kind Observed-score association. “Stress and wellbeing are moderately related (r = −.36).”
Question and intended use How closely did the two recorded baseline scores move together among complete pairs?
Target, population, comparison and horizon Pooled, unweighted Pearson correlation across complete baseline stress and wellbeing score pairs.
Unit, observation and missingness rule One baseline respondent contributes both five-item scores, with all ten responses present.
Measurement or outcome definition Reverse item four on each scale, then average the five keyed responses for each score.
Design and assignment or sampling rule Paired measurements on the same occasion. The offer assignment is inapplicable to this descriptive correlation.
Estimation and precision Correlate the two keyed means across respondents. This summarizes the observed pairs without a population interval.
Verification and source versions Chapter 10 traces the exact keyed sums and three-route result through to the labeled reader sentences. Contract B11-V10-TRACE-v1. Evidence and reproduction checks.
Transport, if the sentence travels Incomplete-score respondents and other occasions or settings need their own coverage and measurement evidence.
Limiting evidence The observed score association supports a statement about the scores. Underlying states require a measurement argument for both scales.

S5 rewrite. Among 922 Tonmick baseline respondents with both five-item scores complete, the recorded stress and wellbeing scores had a Pearson correlation of −0.363.

Next study or check. Collect a second measure for each construct at the same baseline occasion and examine whether shared measurement errors could account for the observed association.

S6. Where did the forecast work?

Field Completed audit
Original sentence and claim kind Prediction on later records. “A model built from entry records identifies students at risk of withdrawal.”
Question and intended use How well did the frozen enrollment model score on new eligible records under its declared policy?
Target, population, comparison and horizon The later simulated Tonmick cohort under no offer. Forecast withdrawal before transfer by month 12 from enrollment information.
Unit, observation and missingness rule One entrant with one frozen probability and a matched known outcome. Transfer first is a known competing exit.
Measurement or outcome definition A withdrawal probability compared with a constant set at the development prevalence.
Design and assignment or sampling rule Freeze the enrollment rule and comparator using development evidence. Score both on the same later cases without refitting.
Estimation and precision Brier loss evaluates squared probability error. AUC measures ranking. The mean forecast was 17.27% compared with 18.20% observed.
Verification and source versions Chapter 8 checks the frozen inputs, matching, scores and calibration in three routes. Contract B11-P8-FROZEN-v1. Evidence and reproduction checks.
Transport, if the sentence travels Even the held-out college groups belong to the same simulated system. A new policy or site needs its own evaluation.
Limiting evidence Good comparative loss and ranking leave local calibration and the benefit of outreach to be checked.

S6 rewrite. On 100,000 later simulated Tonmick records under no mentoring offer, the frozen enrollment forecast had a Brier score of 0.1377 versus 0.1489 for the fixed development comparator. Its AUC was 0.689 for withdrawal before transfer by month 12.

Next study or check. Before rollout at a new college, score the frozen rule and comparator on matched later outcomes and check calibration where the office plans to act.
