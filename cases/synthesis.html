What the observed grades support

The comparison uses 1,116 observed month-12 GPAs from the original 1,219 randomized baseline respondents in 16 fictional Tonmick colleges. The within-college offer minus no-offer contrasts range from −0.160 to +0.330 GPA points. The mean of the college contrasts, with equal weight per college, is +0.085. The common Book 9 slope is +0.085.

The working random-effects mean is +0.079 and its approximate 95% interval is −0.004 to +0.163. The estimated between-college standard deviation is 0.094. The approximate prediction interval for the underlying contrast in a comparable college is −0.139 to +0.298. These are comparisons among observed grades in one fictional study. They depend on the working distribution and the grade observation rule.

Reproduce the hand check
