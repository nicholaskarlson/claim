The Book 7 enrollment model forecast withdrawal by month 12 before transfer under no mentoring offer. All 100,000 later fictional students across 40 colleges had known labels. The archive recorded 18,204 withdrawals and 2,860 competing transfers.

The model’s Brier score was 0.1377 compared with 0.1489 for the constant from development (177 / 984). Model AUC was 0.689, compared with 0.500 for the constant. The mean forecast was 17.27%, and the observed withdrawal proportion was 18.20%.

The aggregate receipt includes scores for continuing, previously-new and unseen colleges. These finite results describe the frozen simulation cohort and its endpoint. A change in the outcome, student mix or transfer process calls for another evaluation.
