# Learning-route fixtures

Invented records only. At the month-three landmark seven remain eligible, six have a screen, four have known labels, and each cell is one. Among two unknown scorable labels, one screen is positive. Bounds on sensitivity and PPV are 1/3 to 2/3. The eighth record ends exactly at month three and must be excluded.

The symmetric normal example has posterior mean zero, variance 1/2 and BF01 = sqrt(2). Invalid duplicate identities, invalid screens, nonpositive SE/prior SD and nonfinite settings must fail. With an extreme hit or false-alarm proportion the uncorrected probit index is undefined; the gate verifies that boundary.
